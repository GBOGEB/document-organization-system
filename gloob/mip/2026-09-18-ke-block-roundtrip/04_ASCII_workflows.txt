NURTURING MIRROR
================
MASTER is not the enemy.
CANDIDATE is not trusted because it resembles MASTER.
The loop learns from:
  source truth + process experience + test evidence + human correction.
Knowledge may grow beyond the static MASTER while source authority remains explicit.
