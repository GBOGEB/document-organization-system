## AOD

AOD = **Architecture–Orchestration–Decision**.

### Architecture

Owns outward material-property/dashboard artifacts, deterministic NIST-regression logic, indexes/navigation and release-facing worker outputs.

### Orchestration

Local: `materials SSOT -> JS calculation/runtime -> tests -> outward artifact/index`. Federated: exact-SHA/result/artifact receipts may be routed to KEB/DOW/child through the hub.

### Decision authority

May validate its own numerical/runtime/outward-artifact consistency. Must not transform independent/reference calculations into bidder evidence or QPS compliance, and must preserve source/provenance class in any federated receipt.

## DMAIC

- **Define:** bind property/artifact role, native runtime/tests, source/authority rules and the fixed 12-gate denominator.
- **Measure:** execute the Level-1 census at exact head and record gate state, native-runtime visibility and deterministic artifact/test receipts.
- **Analyze:** prioritize MIP gaps first; PCA/BT only consume measured observations or explicit comparisons and remain subordinate to NIST/physics regression.
- **Improve:** repair the smallest executable gap while reusing the mature JS/material-property engine, tests and file indexes.
- **Control:** exact-head CI compiles and exercises census/MIP/orchestration, proves no-input analytics DEFER, runs self-test and uploads receipts. The first red invariant becomes the next recursive repair.

MIP = **Modernize (repair/reuse), Innovate (new useful nodes/edges/functions), Perpetuate (repeat exact-SHA execution and receipts).**

## PCA

PCA is a measured multivariate priority diagnostic, never a material-property validator. `python level1/runtime.py pca --input rows.json` requires real numeric observations; inadequate or zero-variance input DEFERs. Synthetic self-test rows prove the implementation only. PCA may prioritize test/feature/work allocation, but NIST equations/regression and controlled source provenance remain authoritative for physical correctness.

## BT

Bradley–Terry is an observed pairwise priority diagnostic using explicit `[winner, loser]` comparisons. No comparisons DEFER. BT may rank repairs, UI alternatives or work priorities; it cannot vote a physical result correct, replace regression evidence, promote bidder evidence or change child disposition.
